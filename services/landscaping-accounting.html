WHERE THE FINANCIAL SYSTEM BREAKS DOWN

Six problems we would look for first.

Recurring work and projects behave differently

Separate meaningful service lines so pricing and staffing decisions use the right economics.

Crew labor drifts without visibility

Compare labor and direct cost trends to revenue without forcing impractical time tracking.

Seasonality creates cash pressure

Use a short-term cash forecast before payroll, equipment and offseason commitments hit.

Materials, fuel and repairs disappear into broad expense accounts

Organize direct and operating costs so margin changes can be explained.

Customers pay after the work is already funded

Improve billing cadence, deposits where appropriate, AR follow-up and cash planning.

Equipment purchases are reactive

Keep debt, repairs and replacement needs visible before they become emergency decisions.
