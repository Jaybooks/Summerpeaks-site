WHERE THE FINANCIAL SYSTEM BREAKS DOWN

Six problems we would look for first.

Management-company and property activity gets blurred

Keep the operating company's revenue, payroll and overhead clearly separated from property-level activity.

Owner reporting takes too long

Standardize close and reconciliation steps so statements are built from reviewed records.

Held balances are hard to explain

Create documented reconciliation and review for accounts that hold funds or require separate tracking.

Fee revenue is difficult to reconcile

Tie management fees and other earned revenue back to operational records.

AP/maintenance workflows create timing noise

Centralize invoice intake, approvals and payment visibility.

Growth adds doors faster than finance processes scale

Make the workflow repeatable before portfolio growth multiplies cleanup.
