WHERE THE FINANCIAL SYSTEM BREAKS DOWN

Six problems we would look for first.

Billing and collections are inconsistent

Create a reliable cycle from time/case activity to invoice, follow-up, cash application and AR review.

Operating cash is hard to predict

Connect expected collections with payroll, case costs, vendors, debt and owner distributions.

Case costs lack visibility

Track reimbursable or direct case-related costs consistently enough to understand exposure and billing status.

Month-end is too manual

Standardize reconciliations, close tasks and financial review so reports arrive while they are useful.

Trust-account workflows require discipline

Keep operating accounting separate from trust-account procedures and align the workflow with applicable jurisdictional requirements.

Practice economics are unclear

Where data supports it, review profitability by practice area, matter type or attorney capacity without forcing artificial precision.
