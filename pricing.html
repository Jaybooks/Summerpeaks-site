FAQ

Do you need access to my bank login?

No. bookkeeping services connects through bank feeds. You can invite our team as an accountant user and keep your bank login private.

How fast can you start?

Once access is granted and we confirm the plan, we can usually begin quickly. If cleanup work is needed, we’ll outline the order of operations so you know exactly what’s happening and when.

Will my reports be clean enough for taxes?

Yes. The goal is clear categorization and reconciled accounts so your CPA can pull what they need quickly—without a scramble at year end.
