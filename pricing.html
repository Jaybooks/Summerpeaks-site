FAQ

Do you need access to my bank login?

No. QuickBooks connects through bank feeds. You can invite me as an accountant user and keep your bank login private.

How fast can you start?

Once access is granted and I confirm the plan, I can usually begin quickly. If you have cleanup work, I will outline the order of operations so you know exactly what is happening.

Will my reports be clean enough for taxes?

Yes. The goal is clear categorization and reconciled accounts so your CPA can pull what they need without hunting through messy data.
