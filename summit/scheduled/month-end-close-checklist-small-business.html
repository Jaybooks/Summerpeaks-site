Month-End Close Checklist: 12 Checks That Make Small-Business Financials More Trustworthy

A practical 12-step month-end close checklist for cash, receivables, payables, payroll, debt, assets, revenue, expenses and management review.

The goal of month-end close is not perfection. It is a repeatable point at which management can trust that the major accounts have been reconciled, obvious errors are resolved and the financial statements are ready to use.

1. Reconcile bank accounts

Resolve missing, duplicate and timing items rather than forcing the reconciliation.

2. Reconcile credit cards and payment processors

Confirm activity, fees, refunds and settlement timing.

3. Review accounts receivable

Apply cash, investigate credits and identify material past-due balances.

4. Review accounts payable

Enter missing bills, apply vendor credits and identify obligations due soon.

5. Reconcile payroll

Compare wages, employer taxes, benefits and liabilities with payroll reports.

6. Reconcile debt

Separate principal and interest and agree balances to lender statements.

7. Review fixed assets and large purchases

Identify additions, disposals and items that should not simply sit in ordinary expense categories.

8. Review revenue cut-off and customer deposits

Make sure timing reflects the business's accounting method and underlying activity.

9. Review direct costs and gross margin

Investigate material changes that cannot be explained operationally.

10. Review unusual expenses and uncategorized activity

Resolve questions while supporting information is still easy to find.

11. Review the balance sheet for reasonableness

Look for negative balances, stale clearing accounts and old unexplained items.

12. Turn the close into an action list

Deliver the reports with three to five issues management should understand or act on.
