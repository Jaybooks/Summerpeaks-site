The Monthly Accounting Rhythm a Growing Business Should Build Before It Gets Complicated

A simple monthly financial rhythm for reconciliations, billing, collections, payables, payroll, reporting, cash planning and management action.

Growing businesses often add accounting tasks one at a time until month-end becomes a collection of reminders and emergencies. A defined monthly rhythm creates ownership and makes the financial function predictable.

Daily or weekly

Capture transactions and documents, invoice completed work, review cash, monitor AR, and route vendor bills.

Before month end

Resolve missing documents, confirm payroll timing, review large purchases and identify unusual transactions that need context.

Close week

Reconcile cash, cards and major balance-sheet accounts; review AR/AP; reconcile payroll and debt; review revenue and major costs.

Management review

Deliver a small package: P&L, balance sheet, cash view, AR/AP action items and the few business-specific metrics that matter.

Action list

Every month should end with a short list of decisions, investigations or process changes. The accounting function creates value when the close changes what management does next.
