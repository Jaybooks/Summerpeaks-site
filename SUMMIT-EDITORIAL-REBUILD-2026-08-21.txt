INDUSTRY ARTICLE REVIEW
=======================
Construction, restaurants, marketing agencies, e-commerce, electricians, landscaping,
medical/dental, law firms, property management, home services and sports-card inventory
were retained because they have distinct operational/accounting problems. Each received
a more specific owner-dashboard section so they do not read like the same generic article
with a different industry name.

FACT-CHECK / RISK REVIEW
========================
- Tax article is framed as recordkeeping/planning, not a promise of deductions.
- Tax sources point to current IRS Publications 334, 538, 463 and 587.
- Law-firm trust-account language points to ABA Model Rule 1.15 and explicitly notes that
  actual jurisdictional requirements vary.
- Payment-control guidance uses independent verification for payment-detail changes and
  references FBI Business Email Compromise guidance.
- Inventory/e-commerce guidance points to IRS small-business and accounting-method sources.
- General financial-management guidance uses SBA and FDIC Money Smart references.
- Public-facing references to QuickBooks were removed; Summer Peaks remains platform-neutral.

EDITORIAL STANDARD GOING FORWARD
================================
Every Summit piece must have:
- one distinct customer/search intent;
- original, practical information;
- no artificial list length;
- no filler used to hit a word count;
- examples or implementation guidance;
- claims that can be supported or appropriately qualified;
- an article-specific reason Summer Peaks can help;
- no unnecessary referral language that weakens Summer Peaks;
- no public-facing QuickBooks positioning;
- a real editorial reason to exist before it is scheduled.
