BEFORE YOU REACH OUT

Straight answers to the first questions owners ask.

Clear scope, practical communication, and no pressure to buy work the business does not need.

Can Summer Peaks help if our books are months behind?

Yes. Catch-up and cleanup work can restore the records, reconcile key accounts, resolve open questions, and create a controlled point from which monthly accounting can continue.

Do we have to replace our entire accounting system?

No. The first step is understanding what is actually failing. Often the highest-value fix is better structure, reconciliation, workflow, or reporting—not a disruptive rebuild.

Can you help with billing, collections, bills, and vendor payments?

Yes. Summer Peaks supports AP and AR workflows, documentation, aging review, collection discipline, approvals, payment planning, and the accounting that connects those processes to cash.

Will we receive more than basic bookkeeping reports?

When the business needs it, support can include management reporting, cash forecasting, profitability views, variance analysis, and decision-focused financial insight.

Do you work with businesses nationwide?

Yes. Summer Peaks provides remote bookkeeping and accounting support to growing businesses across the United States.

What happens after the free assessment?

We review the challenge, clarify priorities during a complimentary discovery call, and provide a tailored scope and pricing. There is no obligation to proceed.
