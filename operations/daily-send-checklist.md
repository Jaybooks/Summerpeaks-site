# Daily sending safeguards

Before doing any new work, inspect current main, today's Gmail sent messages, Resend broadcasts and Facebook posts. Count already successful sends toward daily totals. Never duplicate another run's work.

## All commercial email

- Stop before sending if the authorized verified business mailing footer is absent from either HTML or plain text. Recover the exact address from previously verified records; do not print it in reports, PDFs, logs or website content.
- Confirm sender, reply-to, exact recipient, public source, prior email and domain history, opt-outs, suppressions, bounces and sending warnings.
- Do not promise a referral fee without separate legality and ethics review and authorization.
- Save actual message IDs and timestamps. Gmail send acceptance does not prove recipient delivery.

## Summit newsletters

- Confirm the article is live and fact/link audited; check every prior broadcast for duplicate title, URL and substantive topic.
- Require newsletter_consent=Yes, active Summit topic opt-in, verified-consent segment membership and no unsubscribe or suppression. Never infer consent from assessment submissions.
- Confirm domain health, the authorized verified footer and Resend unsubscribe functionality in both formats.
- Require the approved linked commercial poster and “Watch the 21-second Summer Peaks commercial” after the summary and before the secondary CTA. Include the watch URL in plain text. The article remains the primary CTA.
- Read back the complete saved draft and verify every gate before invoking send. Stop if any required element is missing.
- Retrieve actual sent, delivered, bounced, suppressed and complained recipient events across all pages. Record aggregate discrepancies explicitly. Save the private verified-send PDF.

## Publication and reporting

- Record the unused university, source URL, date and slug in the same article publication commit; include the article in the sitemap and catalogue.
- Exclude known owner/test inquiries from customer conversion counts. Browser exclusion was canceled on October 14; do not publish that implementation.
- If an earlier run already sent a noncompliant message, report the defect. Do not resend the campaign or contact recipients again merely to repair a footer or content omission.
